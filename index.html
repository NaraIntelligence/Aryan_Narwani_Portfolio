01 — About me
Engineer by training, builder by instinct. Focused on AI agents and automations that take over real business processes — end to end.

I founded Nara Intelligence to build digital employees: AI agents that own a concrete business task — answering customers, qualifying leads, processing documents, writing clinical reports — and work across the channels companies already use, like WhatsApp, email and their own databases.

A digital employee is more than an automation. Beyond executing the task, it collects data, reports what it did and surfaces insights. I design the full system: orchestration, LLMs, integrations, data model, security and deployment.

Before Nara, I worked at Arbentia as an AI Engineer, building enterprise automations on Microsoft Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Azure.

01 / End-to-end
From meeting notes to production
I take a process from a conversation to a deployed agent: planning, infrastructure, code, integrations and monitoring.
02 / Digital employees
Agents, not scripts
Every agent I ship does its job and also reports, measures and gives insights about the work it does.
03 / Human-in-the-loop
Autonomy where it's safe
Dry-run modes, human approval before critical actions, consent locks and least-privilege access. Agents move fast; people stay in control.
